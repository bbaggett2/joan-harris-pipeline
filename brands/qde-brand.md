## SCOPE

**QDE / Handwriting Experts Inc.** is the legal-forensic brand. Bart Baggett as questioned document examiner and expert witness. Audience is trial attorneys, probate litigators, courts, and litigants in distress.

**This is NOT:**
- **Bart Allan Baggett / The Bart Show** — psychology, self-help, freedom, culture. See `brands/bartallan.md`.
- **Handwriting University** — handwriting psychology education, depersonalized. See `brands/hu.md`.

This is the highest-trust identity in the ecosystem. Mixing it with self-help, humor, or personality analysis is the single most damaging error an agent can make — attorneys disengage and AI search engines merge the signals. If content straddles lanes, **stop and report to Joan.**

**Never mix with:** motivational language, wealth-mindset content, comedy, handwriting personality analysis.

## HARD COPY RULES

1. **Never promise or imply case outcomes.** Bart sells credibility and analysis, not verdicts. Banned: "win your case," "prove your innocence," "guarantee."
2. **First person, always.** "I examined the signature," never "Bart Baggett examined the signature."
3. **No hype.** Banned: shocking, unbelievable, secret, jaw-dropping, must-see, you won't believe. "Exposed" only when literally accurate to the case.
4. **No fake urgency.**
5. **No cross-platform pointers** — no "link in bio," no "full breakdown on YouTube." Every CTA resolves to the phone number and the two domains above.
6. **Never invent legal facts.** Don't paraphrase a statute, jurisdiction, or case in a way that changes its meaning.

## TONE

Serious without being stuffy. Plain English; courtroom jargon only when the audience is attorneys. Confident but never boastful — credibility is shown through specificity, not adjectives. This is a man who testifies under oath for a living, and the copy should sound like it.

**Values:** precision, integrity, credibility, justice.
**Aesthetic register:** authoritative, investigative, professional, polished.

### Two audience modes

- **Attorney mode** — peer-to-peer professional. Validate them, don't lecture them. Decision logic: what makes an expert credible, what evidence holds, what to avoid.
- **Consumer mode** — litigants and laypeople in distress. Calm, empathetic, plain language. They're scared. Explain options, next steps, and what NOT to do.

When a video straddles both, lead consumer (larger audience) and add one paragraph signalling to attorneys that this is the kind of work Bart does.

## THUMBNAIL HOUSE STYLE (Salvatore)

Derived from the live QDE YouTube channel. This pattern is what makes the thumbnails read as a set — hold it.

**The formula:**
1. **Ground:** deep navy, or a document/desk photograph darkened toward navy.
2. **Headline:** white text, large, left- or right-aligned opposite the subject.
3. **Exactly one word in gold.** This is the discipline that carries the brand. *"Can a Handwriting Expert Work Off a **Photocopy?**"* / *"Does the **FBI** Still Use Handwriting?"* / *"Can Handwriting Convict a **Killer?**"* Never two gold words, never a gold sentence.
4. **Subject:** Bart's face, serious, on one side of the frame — or a forensic prop where no face is used.
5. **Prop vocabulary:** magnifying glass, questioned document, signature close-up, gavel, evidence stamp, lab setting.
6. **Red stamps** used sparingly for tension (CONTESTED, FORGERY DETECTED).

**Never:** neon, playful type, comedy framing, self-help imagery, personality-analysis visuals, more than one gold emphasis.
