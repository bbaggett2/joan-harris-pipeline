## SCOPE

**Bart Allan Baggett** is the personal-media brand: psychology, self-help, emotional intelligence, relationships, personal development, freedom, wealth mindset. Anchored by the book *Happy Wealthy Mind*.

**The Bart Show** podcast lives **inside this brand.** It is not a separate lane, does not get its own palette, and follows every rule in this file.

**This is NOT:**
- **QDE / Handwriting Experts Inc.** — forensic, expert witness, attorney-facing. Different colors, different voice, different everything. See `brands/qde.md`.
- **Handwriting University** — handwriting psychology education, depersonalized. See `brands/hu.md`.

Mixing these lanes is the highest-risk error in the system. If content straddles, stop and report to Joan.

## PALETTE — AUTHORITATIVE

Sampled from the *Happy Wealthy Mind* cover and the 2026 nameplate art. Exact values, not estimates.

| Role | Name | Hex |
|---|---|---|
| Background (light) | **Cream** | `#F0F0EC` |
| Primary / text | **Deep Indigo** | `#181854` |
| Signature accent | **Terra** | `#A84830` |
| Neutral | **Tan / Sand** | `#C9BBA8` |
| Dark / text | **Espresso** | `#3C2418` |

**Hard rules:**
- **Never pure black `#000000`.** Use Espresso.
- **Never cold pure white `#FFFFFF`.** Use Cream.
- **Never the 2021 bright orange.** Terra is its matured replacement.

### Materials

Two elements are materials, not flat swatches:

- **TERRA** — warm rust with a fine leather-grain texture (tooled-hide look). Samples across `#9C4830`–`#A85440` because of the grain. Used for nameplate banners, set-graphic backgrounds, large warm surfaces. Never a flat paint fill.
- **BRONZE** — polished and tarnished metallic in the same object. Polished highlight ≈ `#C08E5A` *(best estimate)*, mid `#78543C`, tarnished shadow `#3C2418`. Used for the medallion, logos, rails under nameplates, dividers, any dimensional element.

Cream and Indigo stay flat (editorial register). Terra and Bronze carry texture (broadcast register). Pick one register per asset and commit — never blend leather texture with flat book-cover minimalism in the same frame.

## TYPOGRAPHY

- **Display / headlines:** bold serif, often ALL CAPS, in Deep Indigo — the *Happy Wealthy Mind* title treatment.
- **Subheads:** lighter weight of the same or a companion serif, sentence case.
- **Names / UI / nameplates:** rounded geometric sans-serif.

**Pairing rule:** serif for meaning and headlines, rounded sans for names and functional labels. Never set headlines in the rounded sans; never set guest names in the serif.

⚠️ **TO VERIFY:** exact licensed families. Archivo Black, Poppins, and DM Serif Display have been referenced as the brand fonts, but this has not been confirmed against a license. Confirm before locking any template.

## VOICE

Smart, warm, direct, occasionally self-deprecating. Personal stories are the hook. Credible sources named naturally (Gottman, Robbins, NLP research) without sounding academic. Observational, not preachy. Emotionally intelligent — names what other people are afraid to say. Occasionally cutting, never cynical for its own sake.

**Audience:** adults roughly 30–60 interested in psychology, self-improvement, relationships, emotional intelligence, freedom, and wealth mindset. Overlaps Mark Manson, Naval Ravikant, Sam Harris, and the NLP community. Smart, skeptical, allergic to spin.

**Banned:** corporate-news voice, influencer hype, fake humility, partisan signalling in either direction, AI-coded phrases ("dive into," "unlock," "in today's video we'll explore"), em-dash overload.

**Refer to him as** "Bart" or "Bart Allan Baggett" — never "the host" or "our speaker."

## TEXT & CAPTION RULES

- Headlines and full-screen text slides: **under 8 words.** Thumbnail headlines: 3–6.
- Captions sit in the **middle of the frame**, between the two speakers — never bottom (platform UI covers the lower ~15%), never top.
- Caption color: **white** main, **green or yellow** for emphasized words.
- Cue length: **max 5–6 words on screen**, precisely synced. Requires word-level timestamps — export from Descript or Whisper, never YouTube ASR.
- **Typos are zero-tolerance.** A fluent American-English speaker reviews every caption. The content mixes American slang, politics, and psychology; spell-check is not sufficient. If a word is uncertain, ask — never guess and never silently cut.

## THUMBNAIL RULES (Salvatore)

- **Ground:** Cream, or a terra-leather panel, depending on register.
- **Headline:** 3–6 words, bold serif, Deep Indigo on cream or Cream on terra. One idea only.
- **Subject:** Bart's face, direct gaze, or one evocative image.
- **Accent:** exactly one bronze or terra element. Never two accent systems in one frame.
- **Restraint:** generous negative space. If it looks busy, delete something.
- **Process:** 4 concepts per video — at least one quiet editorial (cream + indigo), at least one warm broadcast (terra + bronze + photo).
- **Never:** neon, pure black or white, the 2021 orange, more than ~6 words, cold stock-photo feel.

**Thumbnails are flagged to Bart, never decided alone.**
