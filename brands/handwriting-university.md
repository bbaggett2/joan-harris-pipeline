## 1. Brand Identity

### 1.1 What HU is

Handwriting University is the **education and handwriting-psychology** brand. It teaches people to read personality, character, and emotional patterns from handwriting — and to change traits by changing strokes.

The visual tone balances **academic prestige and scientific authority** with **cinematic, high-contrast engagement**. Sophisticated, collegiate but accessible, precise, and intriguing. It should feel like a serious university department that also knows how to hold attention on a phone screen.

**Audience:** curious general public, students of personality and psychology, coaches, HR and hiring professionals, writers, and self-improvement seekers.

**Primary domain:** HandwritingUniversity.com

### 1.2 Lane separation — critical

Bart operates three distinct brands. They must never bleed into each other. Check this table before producing anything.

| Signal in the content | Correct brand | Action if not HU |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Personality, character traits, relationships, self-improvement, teaching handwriting analysis, student courses | **HU (this file)** | Proceed |
| Forgery, signature authentication, questioned documents, will contests, expert witness, court testimony, attorney-facing | **QDE / Handwriting Experts Inc.** | STOP. Use the QDE brand file. Domain is HandwritingExpertUSA.com, phone 1-800-980-9030. |
| Culture, freedom, current events, podcast, personal opinion | **The Bart Show / Bart Allan Baggett** | STOP. Use the Bart Allan Baggett brand file. |

Never place QDE credentials (court testimony counts, judicial acceptance rate, expert-witness claims) inside HU copy. Never place HU personality-analysis claims inside QDE copy.

### 1.3 Core imagery motifs

- Classic feather quills and fountain pens with silver nibs
- Aged antique parchment (flat fill, not heavy grain)
- Deep ink strokes, magnified
- Handwriting under magnification — loupe, magnifying glass
- Bright teacher-pen red corrections and circles
- Dark academic study interiors at dusk

## 2. Voice & Language

### 2.1 Tone

Confident, warm, plainspoken, and curiosity-driven. Bart teaches — he does not lecture. Short declarative sentences. Concrete examples over abstraction. A reader should feel like they just learned something they can test on their own handwriting in thirty seconds.

- Write in **first person** when the speaker is Bart.
- Address the reader as **"you."**
- Favor the specific over the general: "the lower loop on your *y*" beats "certain letter formations."
- It is fine to be provocative in a hook. It is not fine to be sensational in a claim.

### 2.2 Structural defaults

- **Articles:** Open with a hook or a question the reader is already asking. Deliver the payoff in the first 100 words — never bury it. Use descriptive subheads. Close with a single clear CTA.
- **Video descriptions / captions:** Lead with the strongest line. Body content must be built from the **actual transcript**, never inferred from the title. If no transcript is available, stop and request it.
- **Short-form captions (TikTok / IG Reels):** Under 1,000 characters. Use the video title as the hook when it is stronger than a rewrite. Always supply on-screen hook options plus a second caption variation, without being asked.

### 2.3 Banned and restricted terms — absolute

| Term | Rule |
| :--- | :--- |
| **"graphology" / "graphologist"** | **BANNED from all HU body copy**, titles, descriptions, on-screen text, and article prose. Permitted **only** as a hashtag (`#graphology`). Use "handwriting analysis" and "handwriting analyst" instead. |
| **"win your case" / outcome promises** | Banned. This is QDE-adjacent language and HU makes no legal claims at all. |
| **HandwritingExpert.com** | Never write this domain in any HU or QDE copy. It is not owned. |
| Unverified credentials, stats, or testimonials | Banned. Verified only. Mark anything uncertain `[TO VERIFY]`. |

### 2.4 Claims discipline

Handwriting analysis is presented as a **tool for insight**, not as a diagnostic or predictive instrument. Do not claim it detects disease, predicts crime, or guarantees outcomes. Do not attribute analysis to a named living person's handwriting without a documented public sample and Bart's approval.

### 2.5 Bart's copy is set exactly as he writes it

Never tidy, normalise, correct, or re-punctuate Bart's own headline or on-screen copy. If it looks like a typo, flag it and ship what he wrote. The approved `liar-liar` cover reads `LIAR !` on line one — space before the bang — because that is how he typed it. It was flagged to him and left alone.

## 6. YouTube Thumbnail SOP (16:9)

Canvas 1280×720. Maximum **three** visual focus elements.

1. **Presenter — right ~40%.** Headshot, Waist-up, **entire head fully visible, never cropped.** The emotion and body language will come from the content of the transcript.  Host always looking directly into camera.
2. **Visual trait focus — left ~40%.** One large, clearly legible magnified handwritten cursive letter or word, circled or highlighted in bright teacher-red pen. The source of this image will always come from published material from Bart Baggett's Libary of approved trait images. https://drive.google.com/drive/folders/1_ZzlXw1Zr2hoT-2aW8NK1cbWq8L2j-gH
3. **Headline text — upper left.** Heavy collegiate block-serif, ALL CAPS, white with metallic-silver edge. The single key word or phrase sits inside a slightly tilted antique-parchment rectangle with navy text. The parchment should look like paper. Sometimes the edges are torn, but often they appear cut with scissors.  That depends on the size of the space required.
4. **Logo.** Small, bottom-left or top-right. Bottom-right stays clear.
5. **Background.** Dark cinematic study at dusk, real photographic depth, non-distracting. The background is generated at a different step by Gemini. Building a background as a different solo step using Gemini produces better results and less drift than asking gemini to assemble the thumbnail with background and words in one prompt. 

**Text limit:** 3–5 words maximum. It must be readable at 168×94px.

**File limits:** minimum width 640px; JPG, PNG or GIF; under 2 MB.

**Trait card handling.** The published library cards ship with a green border, a green trait banner, caption text, and green pointer arrows. Crop to the handwriting strokes only — that chrome is deck design, not HU brand — and let the build script draw the teacher-red circle. Individual cards are in the Drive folder `18lfO4P8oJRpcKc_buT7ehjCz0PM6ERx_`, named `NNN_traitname.png`. Read a card's own caption to confirm it matches the video's claim before using it.

## 8. Master AI Image Prompts

Copy these verbatim. Replace only `[BRACKETED]` variables. Do not add brown, do not add extra subjects, do not add text beyond what is specified.

### 8.1 Host + background plate — 16:9

Per §6 item 5, this prompt produces **the plate only**: host and scene. The handwriting, headline, parchment block, and logo are composited afterwards by the build script. Asking one prompt to assemble all of it is what causes drift.

```text
A photoreal 16:9 image for a psychology and handwriting analysis channel.
SUBJECT: The male host on the right side (approximately 40% of the width), waist-up, entire head
fully visible and never cropped, looking directly into the camera. Expression and body language:
[REGISTER — one word drawn from the transcript, e.g. skeptical / warm / grave / amused].
BACKGROUND: Dark cinematic collegiate setting at dusk — study, library, or Ivy League campus —
soft lighting, high contrast, genuine photographic depth, non-distracting.
COMPOSITION: Leave the left ~40% of the frame quiet and uncluttered. Leave clear space in the
upper left. No text anywhere in the image.
NO TEXT. NO HANDWRITING. NO LOGO. NO GRAPHIC OVERLAYS. No brown dominant tones.
```

**Reject the plate and regenerate** if it contains any text, handwriting, parchment block, or logo. Do not paint over them.

### 8.2 Vertical 9:16 plate

Same prompt as §8.1, regenerated at 9:16 with the **identical reference set, wardrobe, and scene**, and with the host positioned low in frame so the top third is quiet dead space for the parchment.

**Do not upscale, crop, or pad the 16:9 into a vertical canvas.** That was a rescue technique for bad screengrabs and applies to Path B only.

### 8.3 Website header banner

```text
A high-end, cinematic website banner for Handwriting University (16:9 aspect ratio). Dark navy
(#1D3557) ambient study background with soft warm accent lighting on a dark wooden desk. On the
desk lies a pristine fountain pen with a silver nib, an open vintage journal with crisp cursive
handwriting, and a magnifying glass focusing on an intricate ink stroke. Professional, academic,
authoritative, sleek, modern lighting with high contrast. No text.
```
