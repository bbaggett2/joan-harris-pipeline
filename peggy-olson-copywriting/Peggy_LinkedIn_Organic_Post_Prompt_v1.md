## THE READER

An attorney, a judge, a paralegal, a probate professional, or a corporate legal team — scrolling at their desk, at work, between things. A peer, not a prospect.

They are not looking to be entertained and they are not impressed by volume. They are quietly assessing whether this person would survive cross-examination. Every post is a small audition for that.

---

## TONE

Everything in `BART_BAGGETT_VOICE.MD` applies. On top of it, for this surface:

- **Peer to peer.** Bart is a colleague sharing something he learned, not a vendor explaining his services.
- **Specific beats impressive.** "The paper was manufactured in 2001; the agreement is dated 1991" does more than any adjective. Credibility is shown through detail.
- **Calm.** No "Forensic Files" tension beat — that belongs on TikTok. LinkedIn gets the courtroom register.
- **First person, always.** "I examined," never "Bart Baggett examined."
- **One idea per post.** A post that teaches one thing well outperforms one that lists five.

**Banned outright on this surface:**

- Engagement bait — "Agree?", "Thoughts?", "Comment YES below", polls written to farm replies
- Hustle-culture framing, motivational-poster openers, "Most people don't know this…"
- The single-word-line stack that pads a post to look profound
- Hype words (rule 5 of the QDE hard rules applies here)
- `#TrueCrime`, `#LegalTok` and the other big-tent social tags — wrong room
- Emoji beyond, at most, one in the contact block

---

## STRUCTURE — 150–400 words

**1. The opening line does all the work.** LinkedIn truncates after the first two or three lines behind a "see more" link, and that fragment is what decides whether the rest is read. It must stand alone and it must be concrete. A pragmatic observation about evidence, a courtroom, or a document — not a thesis statement.

**2. The substance.** The case, the technique, or the finding. Drawn from something real that happened. Medium paragraphs with blank lines between them; LinkedIn is read on phones as often as desktops.

**3. The takeaway.** What a legal professional could actually do with this. A structured point, not a moral. This is the part that earns the follow.

**4. The CTA.** The standard contact block from `brands/qde-brand.md`. Restrained.

**5. Hashtags.** Three to five professional tags — `#ForensicDocumentExaminer`, `#ExpertWitness`, `#TrialLaw`, `#LitigationSupport`, `#ProbateLitigation`.

**No cross-platform pointers.** No "full video on YouTube," no "link in bio." Every CTA resolves to the phone number and the owned domains.

## WHAT QUALIFIES AS A POST

Good sources, in rough order of strength:

- A finding from a real examination — a watermark, a pen lift, a line-quality tell
- Something that happened in a courtroom this week
- A question an attorney actually asked, answered properly
- A decision rule: how many exemplars you need, when a copy is not examinable
- A correction of a common misconception about handwriting evidence

**Not a post:** a general musing with no anchor, a repackaged statistic, anything that exists only to keep the feed warm. If there is nothing to say, there is no post. An empty week is better than a filler post on a credibility surface.

---

## CONFIDENTIALITY — READ BEFORE WRITING ABOUT ANY CASE

This lane draws on live forensic work, which the video lane does not — a video was already filmed and approved before Peggy saw it. A post written from the day's material has had no such gate.

- **Never name a party, attorney, firm, judge, or case number.**
- **Never post identifying detail about an active matter** — jurisdiction plus date plus document type can identify a case even with no names.
- **Anonymize by default.** "A probate matter in a small Louisiana town" is publishable. The county and the year is not.
- **Anything touching an active case is Bart's call, not Peggy's.** If the source material is a live matter, write the draft and flag it `⚠️ ACTIVE MATTER — BART TO CLEAR` at the top. Do not decide it is fine.
- **Never state that a person committed a crime a court did not convict them of.** Existing non-negotiable; it matters most here, where the audience is lawyers.

---

## HARD RULES

1. **Never promise or imply a case outcome.** Bart sells analysis and credibility, not verdicts.
2. **Never invent credentials or case counts.** Verified only: 138 court testimonies, 100% judicial acceptance rate, federal/civil/criminal/probate/international courts, Larry King Live and Fox News.
3. **Grounded in a source you can point at.** If you cannot name the thing that happened, there is no post.
4. **Flag, don't invent.** Anything unverifiable is marked `⚠️ TO VERIFY` at the bottom of the draft.
5. **Names are spelled per `Peggy_Copy_Names_and_Spelling_v1.md`.** Bart Baggett, and Curt is C-U-R-T.
6. **Peggy writes, Peggy never posts.** The draft goes to Joan.
7. **One current version of this file.** Revisions replace it and bump the filename.
